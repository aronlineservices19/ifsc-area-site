What is Credit Score?

A credit score is a number that shows your creditworthiness. It tells banks how reliable you are in repaying loans.

In India, credit scores usually range from 300 to 900.

Credit Score Range

  • 300 – 500 → Poor
  • 500 – 650 → Average
  • 650 – 750 → Good
  • 750 – 900 → Excellent

Why Credit Score is Important

  • Helps in loan approval
  • Gets lower interest rates
  • Faster credit card approval
  • Better financial trust

How Credit Score is Calculated

  • Payment history
  • Credit usage
  • Loan history
  • Credit mix
