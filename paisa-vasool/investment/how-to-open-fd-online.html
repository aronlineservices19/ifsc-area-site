How to Open Fixed Deposit (FD) Online

Fixed Deposit (FD) is a safe investment option where you deposit money for a fixed period and earn interest.

Many banks now allow customers to open FD online using mobile banking and net banking.

Requirements to Open FD Online

  • Active bank account
  • Net banking or mobile banking access
  • Sufficient account balance

Steps to Open FD Online

  • Login to net banking or banking app
  • Select “Open FD” option
  • Enter deposit amount
  • Select FD tenure
  • Choose interest payout option
  • Confirm FD creation

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you invest ₹50,000 in FD for 2 years, the bank pays fixed interest and returns maturity amount after tenure ends.

Benefits of FD

  • Safe investment
  • Guaranteed returns
  • Flexible tenure options
  • Easy online opening

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I open FD online?

Yes, many banks allow full online FD creation.

Is FD safe?

FD is considered one of the safest investment options.

Can FD be closed early?

Yes, but penalty may apply.

Does FD give fixed returns?

Yes, FD usually offers fixed interest returns.
