How to Close FD Before Maturity

Fixed Deposits (FDs) can usually be closed before maturity if you need money urgently.

This is called premature FD closure.

What is Premature FD Closure?

Premature closure means withdrawing FD amount before the selected tenure ends.

Ways to Close FD

  • Net banking
  • Mobile banking app
  • Bank branch visit

Steps to Close FD Online

  • Login to net banking or banking app
  • Select fixed deposit section
  • Choose FD account
  • Select “Close FD” option
  • Confirm closure request

Learn here: How to Activate Net Banking

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you opened FD for 5 years but need money after 1 year, you may close FD early and receive amount with reduced interest.

Penalty on Early FD Closure

  • Banks may reduce interest rate
  • Penalty charges may apply
  • Rules vary between banks

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can FD be closed before maturity?

Yes, many banks allow premature FD closure.

Will I lose interest if FD is closed early?

Yes, banks may reduce interest or apply penalty.

Can I close FD online?

Yes, many banks support online FD closure.

Is there penalty for premature FD withdrawal?

Usually yes, depending on bank rules.
