How EMI is Calculated

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. When you take a loan from a bank, you repay the loan through monthly payments called EMI.

Each EMI contains two parts:

  • Principal amount (loan amount)
  • Interest charged by the bank

What is the EMI Formula?

Banks use a mathematical formula to calculate EMI based on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

The EMI formula is:

EMI = P × R × (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N − 1)

Where:

  • P = Loan amount
  • R = Monthly interest rate
  • N = Number of monthly installments

Example of EMI Calculation

Suppose you take a loan of ₹1,00,000 at 10% interest for 2 years.

The bank calculates the EMI based on the interest rate and loan duration. Your monthly EMI will include both principal and interest.

Longer loan tenures usually reduce EMI but increase total interest paid.

Factors That Affect EMI

  • Loan amount
  • Interest rate
  • Loan tenure
  • Type of loan

Higher loan amounts or higher interest rates will increase the EMI amount.

Tips Before Taking a Loan

  • Always calculate EMI before applying for a loan
  • Choose a comfortable EMI amount
  • Avoid taking multiple loans at the same time

Understanding EMI helps you manage loans responsibly and avoid financial stress.
