Home Loan Basics – Beginner Guide

A home loan is a loan offered by banks and financial institutions to help people purchase or build a house. Since property prices are high, most people use home loans to buy homes.

The borrower repays the loan amount in monthly installments called EMI.

Key Features of Home Loans

  • Large loan amount
  • Long repayment period (10–30 years)
  • Lower interest rates compared to personal loans
  • Property acts as collateral

What is Home Loan EMI?

Home loan EMI is the monthly payment you make to the bank to repay your loan.

EMI consists of:

  • Principal repayment
  • Interest payment

Factors That Affect Home Loan Approval

  • Credit score
  • Income stability
  • Employment history
  • Existing debts

Banks usually prefer borrowers with a strong credit score and stable income.

Advantages of Home Loans

  • Helps purchase property
  • Long repayment period
  • Tax benefits in many cases

Things to Check Before Taking a Home Loan

  • Interest rate
  • Processing fees
  • Loan tenure
  • Prepayment rules

Understanding these factors helps avoid financial stress in the future.
