How Personal Loan Works in India

A personal loan is an unsecured loan offered by banks and financial institutions. It allows people to borrow money without providing collateral.

Personal loans are commonly used for medical expenses, education, home renovation and emergencies.

Key Features of Personal Loans

  • No collateral required
  • Quick approval
  • Flexible loan amount
  • Repayment through EMI

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the amount you pay every month to repay your loan.
