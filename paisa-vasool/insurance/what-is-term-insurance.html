What is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is a type of life insurance that provides financial protection to your family for a specific period of time. If the insured person passes away during the policy term, the insurance company pays a fixed amount called the sum assured to the nominee.

Why Term Insurance is Important

Term insurance helps protect your family financially in case of unexpected events. It ensures that your family can manage daily expenses, loans and other financial needs.

  • Provides financial security for your family
  • Affordable premiums
  • Large coverage amount

Key Features of Term Insurance

  • Pure life insurance protection
  • No investment component
  • Lower premium compared to other life insurance policies
  • Coverage for a fixed term such as 10, 20 or 30 years

Who Should Buy Term Insurance?

Term insurance is suitable for people who have financial dependents such as spouse, children or parents.

It is especially useful for:

  • Working professionals
  • Parents with children
  • People with loans or financial responsibilities

Things to Check Before Buying Term Insurance

  • Insurance coverage amount
  • Policy duration
  • Insurance company reputation
  • Claim settlement ratio
