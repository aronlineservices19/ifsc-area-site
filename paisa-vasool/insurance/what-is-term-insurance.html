What is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is a life insurance plan that provides financial protection to your family.

If the insured person passes away during the policy period, the insurance company pays a fixed amount to the nominee.

Why Term Insurance is Important

  • Financial protection for family
  • Affordable premium
  • High coverage amount

Key Features

  • Pure protection plan
  • No investment component
  • Fixed coverage period

Who Should Buy Term Insurance?

Anyone with financial dependents should consider term insurance.
