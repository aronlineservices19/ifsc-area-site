Health Insurance Basics

Health insurance is a type of insurance that covers medical expenses such as hospital bills, treatments and surgeries.

It helps reduce financial burden during medical emergencies.

Why Health Insurance is Important

  • Protects against high medical costs
  • Ensures quality treatment
  • Provides financial security

How Health Insurance Works

You pay a yearly premium to the insurance company.

In return, the company covers your medical expenses as per the policy.

Types of Health Insurance

  • Individual health insurance
  • Family floater plan
  • Group insurance

Things to Check Before Buying

  • Coverage amount
  • Network hospitals
  • Waiting period
  • Claim process
