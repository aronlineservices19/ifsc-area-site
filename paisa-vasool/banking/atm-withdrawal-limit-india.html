ATM Withdrawal Limit in India

ATM withdrawal limit is the maximum cash amount you can withdraw from ATM in a day.

The limit depends on your bank and debit card type.

Typical ATM Withdrawal Limits

  • Basic debit card → ₹20,000 to ₹40,000 per day
  • Premium debit card → ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh per day
  • Limits vary from bank to bank

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your ATM limit is ₹25,000, you cannot withdraw more than ₹25,000 in one day.

Why Banks Set Withdrawal Limits

  • Security protection
  • Fraud prevention
  • Cash management

Can You Increase ATM Limit?

Yes, many banks allow customers to increase ATM withdrawal limit through mobile banking or branch request.

ATM Charges

  • Free ATM transactions are limited
  • Extra withdrawals may have charges
  • Charges vary by bank

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is daily ATM withdrawal limit?

Usually between ₹20,000 and ₹1 lakh depending on bank and card type.

Can I withdraw cash multiple times?

Yes, but total amount must stay within daily limit.

Do all banks have same limit?

No, limits vary between banks.

Can ATM limit be changed online?

Yes, many banks allow online modification.
