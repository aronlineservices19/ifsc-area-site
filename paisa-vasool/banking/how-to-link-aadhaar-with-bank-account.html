How to Link Aadhaar with Bank Account

Linking Aadhaar with bank account helps in identity verification and accessing government banking services.

Why Aadhaar Linking is Important

  • KYC verification
  • Government subsidy benefits
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Easy account verification

Ways to Link Aadhaar

  • Mobile banking app
  • Internet banking
  • ATM machine
  • Bank branch visit

How to Link Aadhaar Online

  • Login to net banking
  • Select Aadhaar linking option
  • Enter Aadhaar number
  • Verify OTP
  • Submit request

Learn here: How to Activate Net Banking

How to Link Aadhaar Offline

  • Visit bank branch
  • Fill Aadhaar linking form
  • Submit Aadhaar copy
  • Complete verification

Example (Simple Understanding)

If government subsidy is linked to Aadhaar, linking Aadhaar with bank account helps receive money directly.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details
  • Registered mobile number

Bank Account and KYC

Aadhaar linking helps complete KYC requirements.

Safety Tips

  • Use official bank website only
  • Do not share OTP
  • Verify Aadhaar number carefully

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Aadhaar linking mandatory?

It may be required for some banking services and government schemes.

Can I link Aadhaar online?

Yes, many banks provide online linking facility.

How long does Aadhaar linking take?

Usually within a few days.

Can one Aadhaar link with multiple bank accounts?

Yes, but one account may be preferred for DBT benefits.
