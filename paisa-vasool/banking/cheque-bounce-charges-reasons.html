Cheque Bounce Charges & Reasons

A cheque bounce happens when the bank cannot process a cheque successfully.

This may lead to penalty charges and banking problems.

Common Reasons for Cheque Bounce

  • Insufficient account balance
  • Signature mismatch
  • Wrong cheque details
  • Expired cheque
  • Overwriting on cheque

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your account has ₹2,000 and you issue cheque for ₹10,000, the cheque may bounce due to insufficient funds.

Cheque Bounce Charges

  • Banks may charge penalty fees
  • Charges vary between banks
  • Both issuer and receiver may face issues

Legal Issues

Repeated cheque bounce cases may create legal complications under banking rules.

How to Avoid Cheque Bounce

  • Maintain sufficient balance
  • Check cheque details carefully
  • Avoid overwriting
  • Use updated signature
