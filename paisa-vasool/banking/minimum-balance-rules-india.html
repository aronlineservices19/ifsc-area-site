Minimum Balance Rules in Banks

Many banks require customers to maintain a minimum balance in savings accounts.

If balance falls below the required amount, banks may charge penalty fees.

What is Minimum Balance?

Minimum balance is the amount you must maintain in your bank account.

The amount depends on bank and account type.

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your bank requires ₹5,000 minimum balance and your account has only ₹2,000, penalty charges may apply.

Why Banks Keep Minimum Balance Rules

  • Account maintenance costs
  • Banking operations
  • Service management

Penalty Charges

  • Charges vary by bank
  • Repeated low balance may increase penalty
  • SMS alerts may notify customers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What happens if minimum balance is not maintained?

Banks may charge penalty fees.

Do all banks require minimum balance?

No, some banks offer zero balance accounts.

Can penalty be avoided?

Yes, by maintaining required balance or using zero balance accounts.

Is salary account zero balance?

Many salary accounts have zero balance feature.
