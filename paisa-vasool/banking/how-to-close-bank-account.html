How to Close Bank Account

You may want to close a bank account if it is unused, has high charges or you are switching to another bank.

Reasons to Close Bank Account

  • Unused account
  • High minimum balance charges
  • Poor banking service
  • Switching banks

Things to Do Before Closing Account

  • Withdraw remaining balance
  • Cancel auto payments
  • Update linked services
  • Download bank statements

Documents Required

  • Passbook
  • ATM/debit card
  • ID proof
  • Account closure form

How to Close Bank Account

  • Visit bank branch
  • Fill account closure form
  • Submit required documents
  • Return ATM/debit card if required
  • Bank processes closure request

Can You Close Bank Account Online?

Some banks may allow partial online process, but many require branch visit for final verification.

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your old salary account is inactive and charging penalties, you can close it and move to another savings account.

Minimum Balance and Penalties

Unused accounts may continue charging low balance penalties.

Safety Tips

  • Take closure acknowledgement receipt
  • Destroy unused cheque leaves
  • Remove linked UPI accounts

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is there charge for closing bank account?

Some banks may charge fee if account is closed within specific period.

Can I close account with zero balance?

Usually yes, after clearing dues.

How long does account closure take?

Usually a few working days.

Should I remove UPI before closing account?

Yes, unlink UPI and auto payments before closure.
