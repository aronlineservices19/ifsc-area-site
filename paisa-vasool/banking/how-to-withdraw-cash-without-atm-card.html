How to Withdraw Cash Without ATM Card

Many banks in India now allow cardless cash withdrawal using mobile banking and UPI-based systems.

This helps users withdraw money even without carrying ATM card.

What is Cardless Cash Withdrawal?

Cardless cash withdrawal allows customers to withdraw money from ATM using mobile verification instead of physical debit card.

Ways to Withdraw Cash Without ATM Card

  • UPI cash withdrawal
  • Mobile banking app
  • Bank OTP verification
  • QR code based ATM withdrawal

Steps for Cardless Cash Withdrawal

  • Open banking or UPI app
  • Select cardless cash withdrawal option
  • Enter withdrawal amount
  • Generate OTP or QR code
  • Visit supported ATM
  • Verify OTP/QR code
  • Withdraw cash

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you forget your ATM card at home, you can still withdraw cash using mobile banking app and OTP verification.

Benefits of Cardless Withdrawal

  • No need to carry ATM card
  • Extra security
  • Useful during emergencies
  • Quick and convenient

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I withdraw cash without ATM card?

Yes, many banks support cardless cash withdrawal.

Is UPI withdrawal safe?

Yes, if used carefully through official banking systems.

Do all ATMs support cardless withdrawal?

No, only supported ATMs provide this feature.

Is OTP required for withdrawal?

Usually yes, for security verification.
