How to Deposit Cash in ATM

Many banks in India provide CDM (Cash Deposit Machine) services that allow customers to deposit money without visiting bank counter.

What is CDM Machine?

CDM stands for Cash Deposit Machine.

It works similar to ATM but is used for depositing cash into bank account.

Steps to Deposit Cash in ATM/CDM

  • Insert ATM/debit card if required
  • Select “Cash Deposit” option
  • Enter account number if needed
  • Insert currency notes into machine
  • Verify amount displayed
  • Confirm transaction
  • Collect receipt

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you want to deposit ₹5,000 into your savings account, you can use CDM machine instead of standing in bank queue.

Benefits of Cash Deposit Machine

  • Quick deposit process
  • No need to visit counter
  • Available beyond banking hours
  • Instant account update in many banks

Things to Remember

  • Use clean currency notes
  • Do not fold notes
  • Verify account number carefully
  • Keep transaction receipt safely

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is CDM same as ATM?

No, CDM is mainly used for cash deposits.

Can I deposit cash without ATM card?

Some banks allow cardless cash deposit.

How long does cash deposit take?

Usually instant or within short time.

Can I deposit money into another account?

Yes, in many banks if account details are entered correctly.
