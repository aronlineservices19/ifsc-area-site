UPI Transaction Limit in India

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) allows instant money transfer between bank accounts using mobile apps.

Banks and UPI apps set daily transaction limits for safety and fraud protection.

Typical UPI Transaction Limits

  • Most banks → ₹1 lakh per day
  • Some banks/apps → higher or lower limits
  • Merchant payments may have separate limits

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your daily UPI limit is ₹1 lakh and you already transferred ₹90,000, you can transfer only ₹10,000 more that day.

Popular UPI Apps

  • Google Pay
  • PhonePe
  • Paytm
  • BHIM UPI

Why UPI Limits Exist

  • Fraud prevention
  • Transaction security
  • Risk management

Bank and UPI Rules

UPI limits can vary depending on:

  • Bank policy
  • UPI app
  • Transaction type

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is maximum UPI limit per day?

Usually ₹1 lakh per day for most users.

Can UPI limit be increased?

Some banks may allow higher limits for specific accounts.

Why is my UPI payment failing?

Possible reasons include limit reached, server issues or incorrect PIN.

Is UPI safe?

Yes, if used carefully and securely.
