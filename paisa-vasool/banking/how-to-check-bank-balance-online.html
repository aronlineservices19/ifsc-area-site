How to Check Bank Balance Online

You can check your bank account balance easily using mobile banking, UPI apps, SMS banking, ATM or net banking.

Ways to Check Bank Balance

  • Mobile banking app
  • Net banking
  • UPI apps
  • ATM machine
  • SMS banking
  • Missed call banking

Check Balance Using Mobile Banking

  • Open banking app
  • Login securely
  • View account balance on dashboard

Check Balance Using UPI Apps

Apps like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm allow balance checking.

  • Open UPI app
  • Select bank account
  • Choose “Check Balance”
  • Enter UPI PIN

Check Balance Using ATM

  • Insert ATM/debit card
  • Enter ATM PIN
  • Select “Balance Enquiry”

Learn here: How to Change ATM PIN

Check Balance Using Net Banking

Net banking provides full account details online.

Learn here: How to Activate Net Banking

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you want instant balance check without visiting bank, UPI apps or mobile banking are easiest options.

Safety Tips

  • Do not share OTP or PIN
  • Use official banking apps only
  • Logout after using net banking

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I check bank balance without internet?

Yes, using SMS banking or missed call service.

Is UPI balance check safe?

Yes, if done through official apps securely.

Can ATM show account balance?

Yes, ATM machines provide balance enquiry option.

Do banks charge for balance enquiry?

Usually basic balance enquiry is free.
