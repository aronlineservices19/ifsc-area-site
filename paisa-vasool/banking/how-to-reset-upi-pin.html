How to Reset UPI PIN

UPI PIN is required to approve payments and money transfers in apps like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.

If you forget your PIN, you can reset it easily using your debit card details.

When You Need to Reset UPI PIN

  • Forgot PIN
  • UPI PIN blocked
  • Changed mobile phone
  • Changed SIM card

Steps to Reset UPI PIN

  • Open UPI app
  • Select bank account
  • Choose “Reset UPI PIN”
  • Enter debit card details
  • Verify with OTP
  • Create new UPI PIN

Debit Card Requirement

You usually need your debit card details to reset UPI PIN.

Learn here: Debit Card vs Credit Card

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you forgot your UPI PIN in Google Pay, you can reset it using your ATM/debit card and OTP verification.

Safety Tips

  • Never share UPI PIN
  • Use official apps only
  • Avoid unknown links
  • Enable screen lock on mobile

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I reset UPI PIN without debit card?

Usually debit card is required for security verification.

Is UPI PIN same as ATM PIN?

No, both are different.

How many times can I enter wrong PIN?

After multiple wrong attempts, UPI may get temporarily blocked.

Is resetting UPI PIN safe?

Yes, if done through official apps securely.
