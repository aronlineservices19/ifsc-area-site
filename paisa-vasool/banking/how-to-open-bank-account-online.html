How to Open Bank Account Online

Opening a bank account online is simple and can be done from your mobile or computer.

Most banks in India offer online account opening with minimal steps.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Mobile number linked with Aadhaar
  • Passport size photo (optional)

Step-by-Step Process

  • Visit bank website or mobile app
  • Select “Open Savings Account”
  • Enter personal details
  • Complete OTP verification
  • Upload documents
  • Complete video KYC (if required)
  • Account gets activated

Example (Simple Understanding)

You can open an account in SBI, HDFC or ICICI within 10–15 minutes using your mobile.

Types of Accounts You Can Open

  • Savings account
  • Zero balance account
  • Salary account

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I open bank account without visiting branch?

Yes, most banks allow full online account opening.

Is PAN card required?

Yes, PAN is usually required.

How long does it take?

It can be done within minutes.

Is online bank account safe?

Yes, if done through official bank website or app.
