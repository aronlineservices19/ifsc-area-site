Zero Balance Account

A zero balance account is a bank account where you are not required to maintain a minimum balance.

You can keep any amount (even ₹0) without penalty.

Who Can Open Zero Balance Account?

  • Students
  • Low-income individuals
  • First-time bank users
  • Anyone who wants no minimum balance requirement

Benefits of Zero Balance Account

  • No minimum balance required
  • Free basic banking services
  • Easy to open
  • Suitable for beginners

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you have ₹0 in your account, the bank will not charge penalty.

This is helpful for people who cannot maintain minimum balance.

Zero Balance vs Savings Account

  • Zero balance → no minimum balance
  • Regular savings → minimum balance required
  • Both may earn interest

Learn more: Current vs Savings Account

Interest in Zero Balance Account

Some zero balance accounts offer interest similar to savings accounts.

Learn here: How Savings Interest is Calculated

How to Open Zero Balance Account

  • Visit bank or apply online
  • Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN)
  • Complete verification
  • Account gets activated

Government Scheme (Jan Dhan)

Under Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana, zero balance accounts are provided to promote financial inclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is zero balance account free?

Yes, it has no minimum balance requirement.

Can I get ATM card?

Yes, most banks provide debit card.

Does it give interest?

Some banks offer interest like savings accounts.

Can I convert to normal account?

Yes, you can upgrade later.
