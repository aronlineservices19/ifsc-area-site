How to Block ATM Card

If your ATM/debit card is lost, stolen or misused, you should block it immediately to prevent fraud.

Ways to Block ATM Card

  • Mobile banking app
  • Internet banking
  • Customer care call
  • Visit bank branch

Step-by-Step Process

  • Login to banking app
  • Select debit/ATM card section
  • Choose “Block Card” option
  • Confirm request

Emergency Customer Care

Most banks provide 24×7 customer care numbers for card blocking.

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your wallet is lost, immediately block your ATM card using mobile banking app to stop unauthorized usage.

After Blocking ATM Card

  • Apply for replacement card
  • Update auto payments if needed
  • Monitor bank transactions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I block ATM card online?

Yes, most banks allow online card blocking.

Is blocked ATM card permanently disabled?

Usually yes. You may need replacement card.

Can I unblock ATM card later?

Some banks allow temporary block/unblock options.

Should I block card immediately after losing it?

Yes, to prevent fraud transactions.
