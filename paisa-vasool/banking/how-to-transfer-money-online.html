How to Transfer Money Online

Online money transfer allows you to send money from your bank account digitally using mobile apps or internet banking.

Popular Ways to Transfer Money

  • UPI apps
  • NEFT
  • RTGS
  • IMPS
  • Mobile banking
  • Net banking

Transfer Money Using UPI

  • Open Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm
  • Select receiver
  • Enter amount
  • Enter UPI PIN
  • Money transfers instantly

Learn here: UPI Transaction Limit in India

Transfer Money Using Net Banking

  • Login to internet banking
  • Add beneficiary account
  • Select transfer method
  • Enter amount and confirm

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you want to send ₹5,000 quickly to friend, UPI is easiest.

If transferring large amount through bank, NEFT or RTGS may be useful.

Things Required for Bank Transfer

  • Bank account number
  • IFSC code
  • Registered mobile number

Safety Tips

  • Verify account details carefully
  • Do not share OTP or PIN
  • Use official banking apps only

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which is fastest money transfer method?

UPI and IMPS are instant transfer methods.

Is online transfer safe?

Yes, if done carefully using official apps.

Do I need IFSC code for transfer?

Yes, for NEFT, RTGS and IMPS transfers.

Can I transfer money 24×7?

Yes, UPI and IMPS work 24×7.
