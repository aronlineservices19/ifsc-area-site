NEFT vs RTGS vs IMPS

NEFT, RTGS and IMPS are electronic money transfer systems used by banks in India.

Each system works differently based on speed, amount and transfer timing.

What is NEFT?

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer) is used for transferring money between bank accounts in batches.

It is suitable for normal transactions.

What is RTGS?

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement) is used for high-value transactions.

Transfers happen in real time.

What is IMPS?

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) allows instant money transfer 24×7.

It is commonly used for fast payments.

Main Differences

  • NEFT → batch processing
  • RTGS → real-time large transfer
  • IMPS → instant transfer
  • IMPS works 24×7
  • RTGS mainly for high amounts

Example (Simple Understanding)

If you want to transfer ₹2 lakh urgently, IMPS may be useful.

If transferring large amount like ₹10 lakh, RTGS is commonly used.

Transaction Limits

  • NEFT → flexible limit
  • RTGS → minimum transaction limit applicable
  • IMPS → usually smaller limits compared to RTGS

Charges and Timing

Many banks offer free online NEFT and IMPS transfers.

Transfer speed and rules may vary between banks.
