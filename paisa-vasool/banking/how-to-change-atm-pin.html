How to Change ATM PIN

ATM PIN is a security code used for ATM withdrawals and debit card transactions.

Changing your ATM PIN regularly helps improve banking security.

Ways to Change ATM PIN

  • ATM machine
  • Mobile banking app
  • Internet banking

How to Change ATM PIN at ATM

  • Insert ATM/debit card
  • Select “PIN Change” option
  • Enter old PIN
  • Enter new PIN
  • Confirm new PIN

Example (Simple Understanding)

If your current ATM PIN is difficult to remember or may be compromised, you can update it instantly using ATM or banking app.

Safety Tips for ATM PIN

  • Do not share PIN
  • Avoid simple numbers like 1234
  • Change PIN regularly
  • Do not write PIN on card

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I change ATM PIN online?

Yes, many banks allow PIN change through mobile or internet banking.

How often should I change ATM PIN?

It is good to change periodically for better security.

Is ATM PIN same as UPI PIN?

No, both are different.

What happens if I enter wrong PIN multiple times?

Your card may get temporarily blocked.
